01 — About

Crafting technology that feels human.

I believe the best technology is intuitive — almost magical. So I don't simply code; I craft experiences.

That mindset is fueled by my experience as a first-generation Haitian-American graduate, a process that taught me an unrelenting passion for turning bold visions into reality. My hope is always to uncover the human touch behind the code.

When I built an AI sports highlight creator, it wasn't just about information — it was about teaching a machine to naturally understand the highlight feeling of a game. With my voice-based grocery guide, the challenge was creating a seamless, forward-predicting experience that simulates a regular conversation.

With my background in machine learning, data engineering, and full-stack development, I love taking creative "what-ifs" and turning them into real, uncomplicated solutions. I want to carry this blend of visionary thinking, intuitive design, and passionate execution to an organization that shares a dedication to building technology that is intelligent — yet, more importantly, deeply human.
